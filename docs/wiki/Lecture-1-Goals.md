# Lecture 1: Goals

![The start page of the finished game](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/055-higher-lower/raw/branch/main/docs/screenshots/lecture-1-goals.png)

The first lecture shows what you will have made by the end of the day: a website that asks you to guess a number between 0 and 9.

## What we gained

- A clear goal: a working website, not only exercises.
- A map of the day: rendering HTML, reading the URL, advanced decorators, then the final game.
- The idea that the guess is part of the URL, for example `/3`.
