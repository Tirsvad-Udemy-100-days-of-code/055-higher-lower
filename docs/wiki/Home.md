# Higher or Lower

Day 55 of Udemy's *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*. You build a Flask website that picks a secret number between 0 and 9. You guess by writing a number in the URL, and the page says whether it is too low, too high or correct.

This wiki follows the lectures of the day. Each page shows a screenshot of the lecture step and explains what you gain from it.
